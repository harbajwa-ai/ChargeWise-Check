Reasonable

Service charge reasonableness check

Enter what's on your service charge demand. Reasonable checks each line against typical costs for a building like yours, and drafts the letter to challenge what doesn't add up.

Under Section 19 of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985, a leaseholder only has to pay service charges that are "reasonably incurred" and of a "reasonable standard." That's the test this tool applies.
